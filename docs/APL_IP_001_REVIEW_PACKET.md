## 5. Chain-of-title review for ООО «Арвектум»

Legal references for the checklist, current as of 2026-08-20: ГК РФ arts. 1228, 1234, 1295, 1296 and 1297. This packet is an engineering/legal-control checklist, not a substitute for legal advice.

Key review rules:

- authorship belongs to the citizen whose creative work created the result; organizational/technical assistance alone is not authorship (art. 1228);
- exclusive rights initially arise with the author and may pass by contract or another statutory basis (art. 1228);
- a written exclusive-right assignment must actually identify and transfer the right in full; where applicable use the rules of art. 1234;
- for employee works, verify that creation was within documented employment duties and that the employer/right-transfer position satisfies art. 1295; do not infer a service work merely from use of company equipment or a general work relationship;
- for commissioned work, classify the actual contract correctly: art. 1296 has specific scope and does not automatically solve every author-contractor case;
- if a work arose while performing another contract whose subject did not directly require creation of that work, review art. 1297 rather than assuming customer ownership;
- material pre-company contributions require a documented basis for ООО «Арвектум» to hold/use the exclusive right if the company was not already the right holder;
- commissioned visual/brand assets require their own rights evidence;
- AI assistance is not treated as a replacement author. The human review must identify the human creative decisions and verify that no unreviewed third-party expression/code was imported.
